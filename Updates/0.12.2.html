AwakeMac 0.12.2

Keep your Mac awake for as long as you need, with a native menu-bar interface and a simple drag-to-Applications installer.

  • Choose Timer, Until, or Unlimited sessions.
  • Enable optional closed-lid protection with configurable power and battery settings.
  • See a clear confirmation when closed-lid protection is active.
  • Receive signed updates through Sparkle; installation waits until your session has ended.

Requires macOS 14 or later and a valid AwakeMac license key. Supports Apple silicon and Intel.
