WakeBee 0.15.3

  • A green dot replaces the menu-bar countdown and On text.
  • Compact, content-sized session panels and smaller Settings. Quit is directly accessible.
  • Closed-lid setup enables the mode after successful macOS approval. Battery use can be configured in one place.
  • Helper registration no longer blocks the UI thread, and idle background work is reduced.

Battery, heat, time, and connection safeguards remain active. End your session and keep the lid open during installation. Versions 0.14 and earlier require a one-time manual migration.
