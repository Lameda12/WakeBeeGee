Untiring 0.13.2

  • The session panel starts with Timer, Until, and Unlimited. Settings and additional options are now in the footer.
  • Closed Lid settings use clearer language, explain when a charger is required, and keep setup details and removal in expandable sections.
  • Timer fields accept numeric input and keep hours and minutes within the supported range.

Existing licenses, preferences, and closed-lid limits are unchanged.

Requires macOS 14 or later and a valid license. Includes Apple silicon and Intel builds. Closed-lid behavior has not been verified on every Mac model or macOS version; check it on your Mac before relying on unattended work.
