AwakeMac 0.12.1 — Test release

The first signed and notarized test release for macOS 14 and later, with Apple silicon and Intel support.

  • Timer, Until, and Unlimited keep-awake sessions.
  • Optional closed-lid protection with configurable battery safeguards.
  • Daily update checks, signed update information, and installation when you choose.
  • Updates wait until keep-awake sessions and closed-lid protection have finished.

A valid AwakeMac license key is required. Closed-lid behavior is still being tested across Mac models.
