WakeBee 0.15.0

WakeBee now uses its own Apple app and helper identifiers. Saved preferences and Polar activations migrate on first launch.

Upgrading from 0.14 or earlier requires a one-time manual install. Keep the lid open, end your session, remove closed-lid setup in the old app’s Settings, disable its Launch at login, and quit it before installing.

After installing, allow Keychain access if prompted, verify your license online, and set up closed-lid mode and Launch at login again if desired.
