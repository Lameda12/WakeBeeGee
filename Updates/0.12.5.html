AwakeMac 0.12.5

Session controls, license activation, and Settings now stay together in the menu-bar popover. No separate app windows open alongside it.

Opening AwakeMac from Spotlight or Applications shows the popover, including after activation. Launch at login stays quiet. The panel cannot be detached into a separate window.

Open Settings in the panel to switch between General, Closed Lid, and License. Use Back to return to your session.

Requires macOS 14 or later and a valid AwakeMac license key. Supports Apple silicon and Intel.
