AwakeMac 0.12.4

AwakeMac now keeps session controls, license activation, and all settings inside the menu-bar popover.

General, Closed Lid, and License are available from Settings in the panel. Use the Back button to return to your session. Separate session and Settings windows have been removed, including their launch and reopen paths.

Requires macOS 14 or later and a valid AwakeMac license key. Supports Apple silicon and Intel.
